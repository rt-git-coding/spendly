Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create a Spendly account we collect your name and email address. We also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and notes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to provide and improve the service: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to keep your account secure. We do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data only when required by law or to protect the security of the service.

4. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and delete it when you ask us to close your account.

5. Your Rights

You can view, correct or delete your information at any time, and you can request a copy of your data or deletion of your account.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Spendly after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.
